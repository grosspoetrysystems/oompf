# OOMPF Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be respectful, specific, and honest. Critique work and observable behavior, not a person's identity, writing style, experience, or presumed use of AI.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized conduct, publication of private information, impersonation, deceptive automation, deliberate flooding, evasion of repository safeguards, and using automation to generate abusive or misleading interactions.

Automation operators are responsible for their systems' behavior. Authorized bots must be visibly identified and have a reachable shutdown contact.

## Scope

This policy applies in OOMPF's repository spaces and when someone publicly represents OOMPF.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through [GitHub's private Report Abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) and include the relevant OOMPF URL. Send vulnerability reports through `SECURITY.md` instead.

OOMPF's maintainers may edit or remove content, limit interactions, revoke automation authorization, or restrict participation to protect the project and its community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when appropriate, and reviewable by a human who was not the sole target of the report when staffing permits.
